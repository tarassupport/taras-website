Support

Need a hand with TARAS? Contact the official support email, or start with the common questions below.

Email support

What to include

  • Your TARAS and iOS versions and device model.
  • What happened and the steps leading to the issue.
  • A screenshot with personal information hidden, if useful.

Do not send your backup password or personal files. Support cannot recover the password of a forgotten encrypted backup.

Common questions

Do I need an app account?

No. Records stay locally in the app. There is no TARAS user account or app data server.

How do I back up my records?

Open More → Backup and restore. Create a backup with a password you keep safe, then save the file outside the app. Saving it to iCloud Drive is optional and is not automatic sync.

Can you recover my backup password?

No. TARAS does not keep the password or key on disk. Support cannot decrypt a backup without its password.

Why am I missing reminders?

Check that reminders are enabled in TARAS and notifications are allowed in iPhone settings. Mileage reminders depend on manually updating the odometer; the app does not read your car automatically.

Are PDF reports encrypted?

No. Choose where you save or share them carefully. Anyone with the report can read it.

What if I uninstall the app or delete all data?

Uninstalling or using Delete All My Data removes local data. External backups are not deleted automatically. Export an encrypted backup outside the app first if you want to restore your records later.
