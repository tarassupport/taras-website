Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the TARAS app and this website handle data, and the choices available to you.

App records and local storage

TARAS stores the vehicle details, odometers, maintenance, costs, workshop names, notes, documents, warranties, photos and invoices you choose to add. Records are stored in SQLite and attachments in the app’s private device storage. Your language choice is stored locally.

The local database and attachments are not protected by a separate app password; they rely on the app sandbox and device operating-system protections. Backup encryption does not mean every local file is encrypted in the same way.

Accounts and connectivity

The app version described by this website has no TARAS user account, app data server, advertising or enabled usage analytics. Your vehicle records are not sent to a TARAS server. Automatic iCloud sync is not provided.

Development previews in Expo Go and app build services are distinct from the standalone app. System and storage services you choose have their own policies.

Photos, files and permissions

The app uses photos and files you select for vehicles, invoices and documents. Notifications are local and require system permission. Optional permissions may be refused.

Current settings do not request location, contacts, camera or microphone access, and the app does not register push tokens for an external server. The system file picker may show storage providers you have added to your device.

Backups, reports and sharing

The app creates an AES-256-GCM encrypted backup using a key derived from your password with PBKDF2-SHA256. Linked records, photos and attachments are included. TARAS does not save the backup password or key to disk and cannot recover a forgotten password. Before replacing data, restoration saves a local encrypted safety backup using the entered password. The total backup attachment limit is 40 MiB.

PDF reports are not encrypted: anyone with the file can read it. You choose where to save or share reports and whether to save backups in Files, iCloud Drive or another provider. That provider’s policies apply to files sent to it. iPhone system backup may include app data depending on system settings and your account.

Retention and deletion

App data stays on the device until you edit or delete it or uninstall the app. More → Delete All My Data requires typing DELETE and an additional confirmation. It erases records and app-managed files, reports and safety backups, and cancels TARAS reminders. If some files or reminders cannot be cleaned up, the app shows a message to retry.

Deletion does not include external copies, system backups or files you shared. Manage those copies with their storage provider. The operating system may retain temporary files or backups under its own policies; secure forensic erasure is not guaranteed. Keep an external backup of important data before uninstalling the app.

Website privacy

This is a static website. It has no accounts, file-upload forms, analytics, ads or third-party font downloads. It does not use cookies or local storage to retain visitor data. The copy-email button only writes the support address to your clipboard when requested.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in. Local app storage does not mean the website hosting provider keeps no connection logs.

Contacting support

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send arrive in the support mailbox hosted by Gmail. Information you send is used to handle your support request. Google’s policy also applies to its email service.

Do not send backup passwords, keys, or personal vehicle records or invoices that are not needed for your request. You can start with the issue description and app and iOS versions without sending your records.
