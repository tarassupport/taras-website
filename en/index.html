Small details. A clearer picture.

Practical tools for your vehicle’s history, upcoming dates and maintenance costs.

01 / TARAS

Your vehicles, together

Add vehicles, photos and odometer readings. Browse them in a stack of cards and select a vehicle to see more.

02 / TARAS

A complete service history

Record service types, costs, notes and an optional workshop name. Attach invoice photos or files.

03 / TARAS

Dates, documents & warranties

Track services by date or mileage, document expiry, part and work warranties, and renewal history.

04 / TARAS

Understand maintenance costs

Review maintenance costs by vehicle, period and service type. Maintenance totals exclude fuel, insurance and document fees.

05 / TARAS

Reports when you need them

Review a vehicle’s record, create vehicle or maintenance-cost PDF reports, and choose where to save or share them.

06 / TARAS

Backups you control

Create a password-encrypted backup with linked records and attachments. Save it outside the app to restore it later.

Local records. Your choice.

No app account is required and automatic cloud sync is not provided. You choose which photos and files to add and where to save your backups and reports.

Reminders to help you keep up

Document reminders are local and need system permission. Mileage tracking depends on updating the odometer in the app; TARAS does not read your car automatically.
